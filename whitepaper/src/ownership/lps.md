# Liquidity Providers


Liquidity providers deploy capital into the pools that borrowers use. Initially there will be one pool that borrowers can borrow from. As our protocol evolves from v1 to v3, we open up the ability for syndicates of LP's to create their own liquidity pool.

A LP receives income based on the following formula 

Transaction  \\( Total Credit Transaction Volume Repaid * 3.5  \\)
