# Credit Reputation

The ability to request future loans will depend merely on the credit reputation for borrowers. We are building a credit score system based in the following parameters: 

**Number of repaid loans**: The score increases with the number of repaid loans.

**History of transactions:** When the borrowers connect their wallet(s), they are sharing the transaction history of those wallets. It may be useful to understand if a borrower can repay a loan based on the volume from their transactions, but this does not indicates is the borrower would pay on time. Still, a healthy volume in transaction may help keep a good credit score.

**Avg. timeframe for past loan repaid:** When signing the initial agreement, borrowers should determine the period in which the loan will be repaid. The borrowers can agree to repay the debt in one of these periods: 1 day, 3 months, 6 months, 9 months, 12 months.

**Number of past collateral liquidations:** Borrowers can increase their credit limits based on trustworthiness, liquidations implies that borrowers do not have the sufficient motivation to repay their loans on time. Can users appeal to this decision, in case that they think is unfair. Users shall prove that their accounts have been compromised.

**Paid amount:** It does not imply whether or not a borrower is reliable. Nevertheless, is one of the factors we consider when determining the credit score. An increase in credit limits suggests various things, such as that the loan has previously been successfully repaid, or that the borrower might have enough money to repay a further the loan, etc.

**Fraud attempts:** In the event of fraudulent activity, the credit reputation score will be reduce and could be banned. Can users appeal?

Borrowers may not be allowed to borrow again if there are any inconsistencies, in one of these aspects. As a recommendation, borrowers should avoid being liquidated.

Lenders which wants to borrow credit, would need to open an account with that role as a borrower.Lending is not an indicator for borrowing.
