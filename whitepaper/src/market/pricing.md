# Pricing Structure

We do not charge any installation nor subscriptions costs to our “users”. 

There are some low gas fees involved in the transactions.

All gas fees shall be covered by the users (borrowers).

Our accepted currencies are: USDC, USDT, DAI, AVAX, ETH and MATIC.

We do charge some transactions fees shown as below:

| Description                                                                                      | Percentage | 
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------|
| Average interest rate                                                                            |      0.13% | 
| A repay interest rate of 21% will be implemented for the borrowers with outstanding balances.    |        21% |
| Credit payment fee rate for lenders and borrowers (included in the gas fees)                     |      0.03% |

**Liquidation policy:** If the loans are not repaid on time and get liquidated by collateral liquidators instead, the borrowers will be penalised with 51% of the overall collateral/borrowed amount.

After each debt have been repaid successfully. The borrower will receive from Lucia a 3.50% cashback from the overall borrowed amount as a reward.
