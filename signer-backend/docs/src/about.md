## Case Studies

### Scenario 1

One common business scenario is sending decks to decision makers inside and outside your firm
Perhaps the deck has financial information on it such as cap table
You protect it with a passcode 
but nothing is stopping others from taking screenshots of your competitive advantage
Or if the deck has confidential information such as list of suppliers
Your trusted suppliers want to know their information is kept safe

#### Flow


1. Alice has raw content and sends content to Bob
2. Alice sends the encrypted message
3. Alice calls Bob and gives secret phrase
4. Bob uses tool following protocol to decrypt
5. Bob reads unlocked deck with financial data

### Scenario 2

As your business grows you realize that your short term memory has been capped. The marginal utility of memorizing yet another additional passphrase starts to take focus and energy away from the company you are creating. 


#### Flow

1. You onboard a secretary
2. You need to give them passwords, physical keys, other credentials so they have access to secured documents
3. In the event that the secretary accidentally loses a key, they are able to notify and ammend
